Collaborative development

Build skills by contributing as a team.

Students participate in collaborative project development under mentor supervision. They learn how to understand requirements, organize tasks, contribute code, review work and communicate progress.

  1. Understand and analyze technical requirements.
  2. Break requirements into smaller tasks.
  3. Manage repositories and Git branches.
  4. Submit and review pull requests.
  5. Collaborate and integrate application components.
  6. Test, debug and document development work.
  7. Present completed work and explain contributions.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Helpful information about our online learning model.

Is this an online or offline program?

This is a 100% online program with live sessions, mentor support and virtual team collaboration.

Will I work with other students?

Yes. Students participate in supervised technical teams and collaborative project activities.

Will I have a dedicated mentor?

Mentor guidance is an integral part of the program. Mentor allocation and support arrangements will be communicated during enrollment.

Will I work on practical projects?

Yes. Students participate in practical, supervised project development and learn professional development workflows.

Will there be daily meetings?

Scheduled stand-ups, technical discussions and Q&A activities are part of the learning methodology.

Will I learn Agile and Scrum?

Yes. Students are introduced to sprint planning, daily scrum meetings, task management, sprint reviews and retrospectives.

Will you prepare me for interviews?

Yes. Resume preparation, technical interview guidance, mock interviews, group discussions and HR preparation are part of the career development approach.

Will I get a job after completion?

We provide career guidance and placement assistance according to the program terms. Employment is not guaranteed. An employment offer letter and subsequent work experience letter are applicable only if you are separately selected and formally employed by the company.

Can beginners join?

Yes, subject to the entry requirements and foundation preparation for the integrated program.
